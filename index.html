About TAEKWONDO ASSOCIATION TAMILNADU

Taekwondo Association Tamilnadu is a state-level body dedicated to developing and promoting Taekwondo across Tamil Nadu. It functions under the framework of India Taekwondo and is affiliated with the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu (SDAT). The association conducts state championships, training camps, and certification programs, enabling athletes from Tamil Nadu to participate in nationally recognized competitions.

About Taekwondo Association Tamilnadu

Taekwondo Association Tamilnadu is a state-level body dedicated to the development and promotion of Taekwondo across Tamil Nadu, functioning under the framework of India Taekwondo and in affiliation with the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu (SDAT), while organizing state championships, that enable athletes from Tamil Nadu to participate in nationally recognized competitions.
